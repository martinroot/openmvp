Support development

OpenMVP tracks real work across the studio: agent runs, API spend and project history. Hosting, domains and model calls are paid out of pocket. Pick an amount and pay with crypto — the tracker and its public pages stay open for everyone.

Accepted coins

BTC ETH USDT USDC DAI TRX LTC BCH BNB POL DOGE

You choose the coin and network on the secure OpenPay / DV.net checkout. The amount stays in USD; the crypto equivalent is calculated there. We never see your wallet or payment details.

What it pays for

Servers, domains, model API calls and the databases behind the activity charts. No ads, no paywall — the public pages stay open.

How it works

Enter an amount, continue to the checkout, pick a coin and network, then send the payment to the address shown. Confirmation arrives by webhook; the order is marked paid automatically.
